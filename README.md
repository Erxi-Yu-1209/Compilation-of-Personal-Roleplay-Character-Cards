## Featured Character Cards

### 01. Next Week If It Rains 2.2

* **File:** `Next-week-if-it-rains2.0-English.json`
* **Adapted From:** The *yuri* short manga *Next Week, If It Rains* (『来週、雨が降ったら』) by Noa Murasaki (紫のあ)
* **Protagonists:** Reiko Takeuchi (27, an office worker preparing for marriage) & Maki Ishibashi (16, a quiet, observant high school senior)

#### Story & Premise

On a rainy April day at a bus stop, Reiko Takeuchi—a young woman on the verge of a conventional marriage—ducks out of the way of a splashing car and accidentally falls into the arms of a high school girl named Maki Ishibashi. Maki lends Reiko her red umbrella. 

When Reiko forgets to return it on time, that borrowed red umbrella becomes the excuse for a quiet weekly ritual: meeting at Maki's room once a week for tea and books until a meeting day finally happens to fall on a rainy day again, giving Reiko a reason to return the umbrella.

This card picks up from Reiko's perspective right after their first parting, filling in the blank weeks leading up to her late-November wedding—a period that passes quickly in the original manga. As the wedding countdown ticks down, every rainy afternoon, small gift, and unspoken thought gradually changes what these weekly visits mean to both of them.

#### What's Included & How to Play

* **A Countdown to Late November:** Each reply features a rainy frosted-glass theme showing the season, weather, location, and how much time remains before Reiko's wedding or the umbrella's return.
* **What Goes Unsaid:** Both women see the relationship differently at first—Reiko thinks she is simply keeping a lonely student company out of sisterly kindness, while Maki is deliberately using the umbrella to keep Reiko in her life. Each reply includes a short subtext line capturing the thoughts neither of them says out loud.
* **Choose Ending or Branch Into a New Route:** The built-in worldbook includes the bittersweet parting and reunion ending from the original manga. However, if you want to steer the story in a different direction—such as confessing early, running away together, or changing their fate before the wedding—you can simply open the card's World Info (lorebook) in SillyTavern and **disable Entry 1 and Entry 3** (`escalation, near-kiss, and severance` and `the ending was shaped by hidden recognition`).
* **5 Starting Scenarios & Clickable Choices:** Includes the April bus stop encounter plus 4 alternate greetings (rainy tea time, receiving a bookmark, the plum-rain season "run away with me" moment, and returning the umbrella on a clear day). Replies also come with clickable action buttons at the bottom if you want quick story prompts.

### 02. The Story of Xiaoxiao and Little Leaf 2.2

* **File:** `The Story of Xiaoxiao and Little Leaf 2.2.json`
* **Adapted From:** A serialized story video by a creator on Bilibili
* **Protagonists:** Qian Yixiao / "Xiaoxiao" (an outgoing, affectionate sweet girl) & Ye Han / "Little Leaf" (a quiet, guarded, high-achieving shut-in)

#### Story & Premise

Xiaoxiao grew up in a warm, loving county-town family where reaching out to others always brought a smile back. Ye Han ("Little Leaf") grew up alone as a left-behind child, learning far too early to lock her door, rely on her computer screen for comfort, and treat uninvited kindness as trouble.

Before high school, when Xiaoxiao tried to offer Ye Han an umbrella and biscuits on a rainy afternoon, Ye Han turned her down flat. Yet once they end up in the same high school class and become deskmates, Xiaoxiao's chatter, borrowed math papers, and shared meals slowly squeeze into Ye Han's tightly guarded world—until Xiaoxiao becomes the sole exception to all of Ye Han's rules.

#### Five Timeline Stages (Automatic Progression)

The story spans five distinct stages from their teenage years in a small county town to their university days in Tianjin. By default, the card starts when they first meet in high school, and the system automatically recognizes where you are in the timeline and transitions naturally as your story unfolds:

1. **Stage 1 — Pre-High School:** Their first encounter on a rainy day before high school, where Xiaoxiao's easy warmth meets Ye Han's closed door.
2. **Stage 2 — High School Deskmates:** Class 1-2 at County No. 1 High School. Borrowing homework under whirring ceiling fans, math tutoring, and bringing breakfast turn a one-sided pestering into everyday reliance.
3. **Stage 3 — High School Cohabitation:** After their mothers reconnect, Ye Han moves into Xiaoxiao's family home. Warm home-cooked meals and shared bedrooms bring them closer under the safe label of "sisters."
4. **Stage 4 — Graduation Awareness:** A brief summer separation after the college entrance exams makes Xiaoxiao realize her feelings aren't just friendship anymore. Terrified that confessing would ruin their life together, she starts holding back—while Ye Han quietly changes her college applications so they can stay together.
5. **Stage 5 — University Cohabitation:** Sharing a two-bedroom apartment off-campus at Hebei University of Technology. Xiaoxiao cooks, Ye Han cleans, and beneath their cozy roommate routine lies Xiaoxiao's careful self-restraint and Ye Han's complete, unspoken trust.

#### What's Included & How to Play

* **Shared Notebook Status Bar:** At the bottom of every reply, the card automatically formats a warm notebook-style page tracking the current stage, Xiaoxiao's **Mood** and **Restraint** (how hard she's trying to hide her crush), Ye Han's **Sense of Security** and **Boundary State** (*Defensive* → *Passive Tolerance* → *Complete Acceptance*), unspoken subtext, and relationship milestones.
* **Jump to Any Era:** While the default opening starts at the beginning of their high school deskmate days, you can swipe through the **Alternate Greetings** to jump straight into borrowing math answers in Grade 10, cooking tomato scrambled eggs in their college apartment, or styling cosplay wigs together in Stage 5.
